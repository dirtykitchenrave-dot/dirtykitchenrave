Not a label. A full-throttle kitchen rave.

DIRTY KITCHEN RAVE is a multi-genre bass label from London, made for bassheads, ravers and late-night misfits. It releases whatever hits hardest on a dancefloor and doesn't care which genre box it lands in.

Label manager Afghan Headspin runs a crew of UK and international producers with deep roots in the Spanish breaks scene. More than 230 drops in, DKR also streams on Twitch, hosts a podcast, runs a Discord and sells its own clothing and vinyl.
